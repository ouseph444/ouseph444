## 👤 The Story

**Physicist by training. Data scientist by practice.**

I spent six years in high-energy physics — designing HPC pipelines for multi-terabyte collider datasets, publishing **15+ peer-reviewed papers** in Q1 journals, and mentoring researchers to their first co-authorships. The tools were the same ones that matter in industry: **Bayesian inference, Monte Carlo simulation, statistical modeling, and machine learning** — applied to problems where getting the answer wrong has real consequences.

Now I bring that rigor to industry. I specialize in extracting signal from noise in large, messy, high-stakes datasets.
